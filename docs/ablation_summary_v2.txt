Ablation Summary (v2)

Disentangling Calibration Failure from Label-Ontology Mismatch

0. Problem Statement
Error analysis of the baseline encoder-only classifier reveals a dominant failure pattern concentrated around the neutral class, characterized by high-confidence neutral↔positive misclassifications and saturated prediction margins. Two competing explanations are plausible:
	1.	Calibration failure: the learned decision geometry is broadly correct, but softmax probabilities are systematically over-confident, causing errors to appear severe despite correct ranking.
	2.	Label-ontology mismatch: the discrete sentiment labels (negative / neutral / positive) do not align with the intrinsic semantic structure of the embedding space, particularly with respect to the role of “neutral”.

The goal of this ablation suite is to causally separate these two hypotheses using controlled, orthogonal experimental axes, without introducing new model architectures or confounding training dynamics.
